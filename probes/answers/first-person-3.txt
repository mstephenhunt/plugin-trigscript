TrigScript: first person, probe 3 - answers
Map: trigscript-first-person-3.scx (the lobby says "TrigScript first person 3")
Single player. Please RECORD THE SCREEN. About 2-3 minutes.
Start in REMASTERED graphics; press F5 about halfway to compare Classic.

What it is: play StarCraft as usual. Your Marine starts selected: right-click on the MAIN screen to move it, A and a
click to attack-move. Scroll the main screen as you like. The 3D view at the top of the minimap is what the Marine
sees: where it stands and the way it faces, read from the game every frame. When it shoots, it faces its target, and so
does the view. The view turns smoothly toward the Marine's facing (at most 4 degrees a frame) and follows its place
a little behind.
Keys: B puts the eye half a cell behind the Marine (over its shoulder, the Marine drawn in the view) and back;
V changes how the monsters look. Nothing else is the script's.
Note: a right-click on the TOP of the minimap (the 3D view) sends the Marine up there, to a place it cannot reach.
Click the main screen, or the arena at the bottom of the minimap.
Same arena and monsters as probe 2, so the two can be compared.
Pictures from the game played without anyone (right-clicks given by the test tool): first-person-3-play.png (a walk
through the rooms), first-person-3-behind.png (the same walk, over the shoulder), first-person-3-steps.png (16 frames
in a row during a turn).
