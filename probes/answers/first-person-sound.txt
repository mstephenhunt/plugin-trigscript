TrigScript: first person, probe 2 (sounds) - answers
Map: trigscript-first-person-sound.scx (the lobby says "TrigScript sound places")
Single player. Remastered or Classic graphics, either. Turn the game's MUSIC OFF (Options > Sound), keep the sound effects on.
Please RECORD THE SCREEN WITH SOUND (the recording's two channels are what gets measured). Leave the mouse in the middle
of the screen and do not scroll. About 85 seconds; it goes round twice, then says "Done".

What it does: the screen stays on the middle of the map. At each step a unit of yours appears at a place and dies a
second later (the game plays its death sound there); 1.5 seconds later the same sound plays with no place at all (a
trigger's Play WAV), to compare. The places: the middle, the left edge, the right edge, a screen and a half off to the
left, the same to the right, three screens off to the right, up the screen, down the screen. A Marine first, then a
Zergling. Each step is printed as it starts.

1. Listening with headphones (the recording answers these too):
  Does the death at the LEFT edge sound from the left, and at the RIGHT edge from the right? ->
  Is the death FAR LEFT / FAR RIGHT (off the screen) heard at all? Fainter? From that side? ->
  VERY FAR RIGHT: heard? ->
  UP and DOWN the screen: any difference from the middle? ->
  The trigger's Play WAV after each: always the same, from the middle? ->
  Do the units' deaths and the Play WAV copies sound like the same sound? ->
