TrigScript: first person, probe 1 - answers
Map: trigscript-first-person.scx (the lobby says "TrigScript first person")
Single player. Classic graphics (Options > Graphics) for a sharp minimap; press F5 once near the end to see it in Remastered's too.
Please RECORD THE SCREEN (the minimap is the picture). About 3 minutes.

What it is: the MINIMAP is the screen. Its top is a 3D view drawn in the fog of war; its bottom is a real arena
(cliffs of the game's own terrain) with your Marine in it. The main screen follows the Marine.
W A S D walk (W toward where the view looks, A D sideways), the mouse turns the view, Q E turn too.
V changes how the monsters look in the view, T changes how the mouse turns.
Zerglings and Hydralisks of a computer chase you (8 at a time, more come). Your Marine cannot die; it shoots when you stand still.
Do not click the Marine: with it selected, A and S are the game's Attack and Stop. If you did, press Esc.
Pictures of what the view should look like, from the game played without anyone: first-person-look-a.png, -b.png, -c.png, first-person-budget.png
(frames left to right, top to bottom; white lit, grey fog; coloured squares are units, which the game shows only where they are lit).

1. Starting
  Does the arena show at the bottom of the minimap, lit, with the Marine and the monsters as dots? ->
  Does the 3D view show walls at the top of the minimap? ->
  Does the main screen stay on the Marine? Any shaking? ->

2. Walking and the view (the mouse turning is "aim" at the start: the view faces the cursor, seen from the Marine)
  Walk with W toward a wall: does the wall in the view grow, and does the Marine stop at the cliff? ->
  Does the view match the arena (rooms, the two blocks, the pillars) as you walk round it? ->
  A D (sideways) and S (back): do they go the right way? ->

3. Turning with the mouse: try both, then say which you would keep
  "aim" (at the start): move the cursor round the Marine; the view turns to face it. Smooth? Too fast or slow? ->
  Press T for "steer": the further left or right of the Marine the cursor is, the faster the view turns (nothing near the Marine). Smooth? ->
  Which turning is better to play, and which looks better on video? ->
  Do Q and E feel useful too? ->

4. The monsters in the view - press V to go through the three looks (it starts on (c)):
  (a) lit silhouettes in the fog: do you see the Zerglings and Hydralisks coming? Can you tell them apart? ->
  (b) green dots (your own Overlords, one a dot) over the view: readable? ->
  (c) both: silhouettes with red dots on them: readable? ->
  Which look is best? ->
  Are monsters hidden when they are behind a wall (and shown again when they come round it)? ->
  When they reach you, they are drawn as big shapes at the bottom of the view. Good, or too much? ->
  At about 12 pictures a second, do they move smoothly enough? ->

5. The budget
  Does the game ever slow down or stutter (with 8 monsters near you, while turning)? ->
  Any monster, wall or part of the arena missing for a moment (the game makes only so many units a frame)? ->
  Any "WARNING: Unit unplaceable" lines, or other messages? ->
