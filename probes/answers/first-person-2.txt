Single player. Please RECORD THE SCREEN. About 2-3 minutes.
Start in REMASTERED graphics (the map is now all Ice, made for Remastered's minimap); press F5 about halfway to
compare Classic, and once more near the end.

What changed since probe 1 ("a bit janky"):
- a new picture every game frame, and the view turns in small even steps (at most 4 degrees a frame);
- your Marine no longer walks on orders: the camera walks by itself at a steady speed and the Marine is put where it is
  (Move Unit), so nothing can block it; W S forward and back, A D sideways;
- the monsters stop 2-3 cells from you and fight from there (Zerglings reach 3.5 tiles, Hydralisks 5), so they stand
  in front of you in the view;
- the arena is rooms, doors and a long hall, with thick walls; a straight wall is one face in the view.
Controls as before: the mouse turns ("aim" at the start: the view turns toward the cursor), T switches to "steer",
Q E turn too, V changes how the monsters look. Do not click the Marine (if you did, press Esc).
