recoloured every frame). The MINIMAP shows the real arena from above: your Marine (yellow) and the monsters (red).

B puts the eye over the Marine's shoulder (the Marine drawn yellow in the view) and back.
The screen is held on the view: do not mind if it jumps back after you click the minimap.
The first second the view builds itself (a row a frame).
