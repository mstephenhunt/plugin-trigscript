Für Elise, the showcase on the cinematic arrangement: a check play
=================================================================

Map: trigscript-fur-elise.scx (lobby "Für Elise"). About 2 minutes 40 seconds, nothing to press.
(trigscript-fur-elise-straight.scx beside it is the same showcase on the straight piece, 3:38, for comparison.)
Stills of what it should look like: fur-elise-showcase-frames.png beside the map (the minimap on the left, the main
screen on the right, as the build works them out — not the game's own drawing).

What to record
--------------
- Host it as a multiplayer game, alone. Remastered graphics, no F5.
- Options: game MUSIC OFF, sound effects ON (the piece plays as a sound effect).
- Record the whole run, from the lobby to the end text and a few seconds after. Don't move the camera or click.

What is where
-------------
- Main screen: a keyboard of Flags, A1 to E7 (40 white keys, the black keys above them). The piano's notes fall onto
  it, right hand gold, left hand cyan; each key lights in its hand's colour while it sounds. Only the piano is drawn
  there, all the way through.
- Minimap: the same notes falling onto a keyboard row. In the storm and the final statement the rest of the band
  (strings, brass, bass, synths, pads) falls in violet too, and the keyboard's front row pulses red on the biggest
  drum hits. The intro and the ending show the piano alone.
- The storm (about 1:12 to 1:36): the right hand's chords red, the white keys flash red on each chord.
- Explosions, with the game's own explosion sounds (times from the start of the game, about):
    0:16  the band comes in (its first downbeat): Wraiths over the melody
    1:14  the storm's first big hit: three Battlecruisers
    1:24  the storm, the middle: Wraiths
    1:33  the storm's last big hit: three Battlecruisers
    1:42  the final statement begins (the theme returns): three Battlecruisers
    2:03  the final, a big bar: Wraiths
    2:16  the final's last big bar: Wraiths
- Text: "Für Elise" at the start; "Für Elise — Ludwig van Beethoven" when the last note is let go (about 2:28).
  After that the keyboard stays and the game waits; leave when the sound has rung out.

What to look at
---------------
1. Do the notes land on the keys as you hear them (not early, not late)? The clip starts two frames before the
   picture, to take up the ~95 ms the visualizer probe measured. ->
2. Does the main-screen keyboard fit in the view, both ends visible (A1 at the far left, E7 at the far right)? ->
3. Do the first text line and the end line show the ü and the dash (—) properly? ->
4. The explosions: the right moments, too many, too few? Are their sounds all right over this music? ->
5. The minimap in the storm and the final: does the violet band read well, or is it clutter? Do the red pulses on the
   drum hits help? ->
6. Are the intro and the ending calm enough (only the piano)? ->
7. Any stray unit, a Flag or Overlord in the wrong place, an error text, a stutter or slowdown? ->
