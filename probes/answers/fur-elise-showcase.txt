Für Elise, the showcase on the trance remix: a check play
========================================================

Map: trigscript-fur-elise.scx (lobby "Für Elise"). About 2 minutes 40 seconds, nothing to press.
Beside it, for comparison: trigscript-fur-elise-cinematic.scx (the cinematic arrangement) and
trigscript-fur-elise-straight.scx (the piece as written).
Stills of what it should look like: fur-elise-showcase-frames.png (the minimap on the left, the main screen on the
right, as the build works them out — not the game's own drawing).

Music: Für Elise by Ludwig van Beethoven (public domain); our transcription from the Breitkopf & Härtel edition of
1888, and our remix. Piano: Salamander Grand Piano V3 by Alexander Holm (CC BY 3.0). Orchestral and percussion
samples: VSCO 2 Community Edition by Versilian Studios (CC0). Made with TrigScript (scmjs.dev).

What to record
--------------
- Host it as a multiplayer game, alone. Remastered graphics, no F5.
- Options: game MUSIC OFF, sound effects ON (the piece plays as a sound effect).
- Record the whole run, from the lobby to the end text and a few seconds after. Don't move the camera or click.

What is where (times from the start of the game, about)
------------------------------------------------------
- Main screen: a keyboard of Flags, A1 to E7 (40 white keys, the black keys above them). Notes fall onto it at half
  speed: the melody gold (drawn on top), the piano's left hand cyan; in the drops the synth stabs too, violet.
- Minimap: the same, plus the rest of the band (arps, pads, strings, bass) in violet during the drops and builds.
- 0:00  the piano intro: calm, the piano alone.
- 0:17  drop 1: the band comes in. White keys flash red, three Battlecruisers explode.
- 0:45  breakdown 1 (calm), 0:52 build 1.
- 1:00  drop 2, the storm, the biggest: the stab chords red, the white keys flashing red on each chord, five
        Battlecruisers on its downbeat; an impact with Wraiths at 1:17.
- 1:29  breakdown 2 (calm), 1:37 build 2.
- 1:45  drop 3: red flash, three Battlecruisers; Wraiths at 2:15.
- 2:16  the ending: the piano alone again, calm.
- 2:26  "Für Elise — Ludwig van Beethoven". The keyboard stays and the game waits; leave when the sound has rung out.
The explosions play the game's own explosion sounds over the music.

What to look at
---------------
1. Do the notes land on the keys as you hear them, in the drops too (not early, not late)? ->
2. Is the melody (gold) easy to follow in the drops, over the violet stabs? ->
3. The storm (drop 2): red chords, red flashes, five Battlecruisers: right amount, too much, too little? ->
4. Are the intro, the breakdowns and the ending calm enough (the piano alone)? ->
5. The minimap in the drops: does the violet band read well, or is it clutter? ->
6. Any stray unit, a Flag or Overlord in the wrong place, an error text, a stutter or slowdown? ->
